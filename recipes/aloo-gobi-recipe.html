Aloo-Gobi-Recipe


Home

Description

Make the best Aloo Gobi's that's simply delicious,flavor packed & healthy! There is nothing more comforting for an indian soul than a bowl of fresh coocked sabxi(veggies) served with some hot fluffy Basmati Rice,puffed rotis or chapati and Dal Tadka. This potato cauliflower curry is a pure comfort food if you love the magical indian flavors.Made with pantry staples,this dish is surely going to win your heart for its simplicity&delicious flavors.

Ingredients

  • potatoes
  • cauliflower
  • spices
  • aromatic
  • tomatoes

Steps

    Step 1:Build the Aromtic Base

  1. Heat 2 to 3 tablespoons of oil in a heavy-bottomed pan. Drop in ½ teaspoon of cumin seeds. Once they start to sizzle, add your minced ginger and garlic, sautéing for just 30 seconds. Toss in the chopped onions and green chili, stir-frying until the onions turn translucent.

    2. Step 2:Par-cook the Potatoes

  2. Drain the cubed potatoes thoroughly and add them straight into the pan. Stir-fry them for 2 to 3 minutes. Put the lid on and cook on low-to-medium heat for 8 to 9 minutes until they are about half-done. (Note: If the pan gets too dry, splash 2 to 3 tablespoons of water so they don't scorch).

    3. Step 3: Introduce Cauliflower and Spice Powders

  3. Once the potatoes are slightly tender but undercooked, add the cauliflower florets and stir-fry everything together for about 3 minutes. Sprinkle in the spice powders evenly:
    • ¾ to 1¼ tsp Red chili powder
    • 1 to 1½ tsp Garam masala
    • ¾ to 1 tsp Coriander powder
    • ½ to ¾ tsp Roasted cumin powder
    • ¼ tsp Turmeric powder

    Step 4:Steam Until Fork-Tender

  4. Mix the spices thoroughly with the veggies. Sprinkle 2 to 3 tablespoons of water across the sides or over the top of the dish to trap moisture. Cover the lid and cook on low heat, checking and stirring every few minutes. Once they are nearly tender, sprinkle ½ to ¾ teaspoon of salt (to taste), cover, and continue cooking until the potatoes are perfectly soft but the cauliflower retains a bite.

    5. Step 5: Blend the Tomatoes and Garnish

  5. Uncover the pan, add your chopped tomatoes, and crush 1 tablespoon of kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves) between your palms into the mix. Crank the heat up to medium-high and stir-fry for 2 to 4 minutes until the tomatoes completely soften, blend into a coating masala, and lose their raw smell.

    6. Step 6: Final Touches

  6. Turn off the stove. Sprinkle ½ to 1 teaspoon of amchur powder (dry mango powder) for a hit of tanginess if desired, and garnish generously with your chopped coriander leaves. Give it a gentle squeeze of fresh lemon juice if you love an extra bright flavor!

Serve hot and enjoy
