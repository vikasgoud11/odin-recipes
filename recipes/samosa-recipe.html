Description

It's hard to resist a cripsy and flaky Samosa filled with delicious spiced potatoes.This perfected chef's recipe will help you make the BEST potato Samosas at home.If you ever wondered how those eateries selling authentic Punjabi foods make the crispiest and flakiest samosas,you get to try this recipe.

Ingredients

    For the Pastry Crust (Dough):

  • 2 cups (250g) Plain Flour (Maida)
  • 4–5 tbsp Ghee or Oil
  • ½ tsp Carom Seeds (Ajwain)
  • ½ tsp Salt
  • ½ cup Cold Water (added sparingly)

    For the Aloo-Matar Filling:

  • 3–4 Medium Potatoes (boiled, peeled, and roughly mashed)
  • ½ cup Green Peas (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 tbsp Oil or Ghee
  • Aromatics: 1 tsp chopped ginger, 1–2 green chilies (chopped)
  • Whole Spices: 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera), ½ tsp whole coriander seeds (crushed)
  • Ground Spices: ½ tsp red chili powder, ½ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp garam masala, ½ tsp amchur (mango powder), and ½ tsp chaat masala

Steps

Step 1: Perfect the Flaky Samosa Dough

  1. Combine Dry Mix: In a large mixing bowl, combine the maida, salt, and ajwain.
  2. The Crucial Crumble (Moyan): Pour in the 4–5 tablespoons of melted ghee or oil. Use your fingertips to aggressively rub the fat into the flour. Keep going until the flour resembles breadcrumbs and holds its shape tightly when pressed in your fist. (Skipping this ensures a hard, non-flaky crust).
  3. Knead Stiffly: Gradually add cold water, a few tablespoons at a time. Knead firmly to create a very stiff, hard dough—do not make it soft or sticky like roti dough.
  4. Rest: Wrap the dough tightly in a damp muslin cloth and let it rest for 30 to 45 minutes.

Step 2: Prepare the Savoury Potato Filling

  1. Sizzle Whole Spices: Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a pan. Add the cumin seeds and crushed coriander seeds. Sauté until intensely aromatic.
  2. Cook Aromatics: Stir in the chopped ginger and green chilies, cooking for 1 minute. Add the green peas and sauté for 2 minutes until tender.
  3. Incorporate Masalas: Turn the heat down. Add the red chili powder, turmeric, garam masala, cumin powder, and salt. Stir for 30 seconds.
  4. Fold in Potatoes: Toss in the roughly mashed boiled potatoes. Using a spatula, stir-fry the mix for 3–4 minutes, pressing down so the potatoes absorb the spice profile.
  5. Finish & Cool: Sprinkle in the amchur, chaat masala, and fresh coriander leaves. Mix well, remove from heat, and let the filling cool completely before using. (Warm stuffing causes steam, making the dough soggy).

Step 3: Roll, Slice, and Shape the Pastry Sheets

  1. Divide: Divide the rested dough into 6 or 7 equal-sized smooth balls. Keep the balls you aren't working with covered under the damp cloth.
  2. Roll the Oval: Take one ball (do not dust with loose flour if possible) and roll it out with a rolling pin into an oval shape (approx. 6 inches long by 4 inches wide). The thickness should be even—neither paper-thin nor thick.
  3. Divide: Use a knife or pizza cutter to slice the oval horizontally right through the centre, creating two matching semi-circles.

Step 4: Fold, Stuff, and Securely Seal

  1. • Form the Cone: Pick up one semi-circle. Lightly brush water along its straight edge. Bring the two corners of the straight edge together, overlapping them slightly to form a neat cone pocket. Press the overlapping seam firmly from the inside and outside to stick it together.
  2. • Stuff: Cradle the dough cone in your hand. Fill it with 1 to 2 tablespoons of the cooled potato stuffing. Leave about a ½-inch border clear at the top—do not overstuff it.
  3. Create the Pleat & Seal: Dampen the entire inner top rim with water. Make a small fold or pleat in the back edge of the dough circle (this allows the samosa to sit flat). Firmly press the front and back edges together, sealing it tightly to prevent oil from entering during frying./li>

Step 5: The Halwai-Style Slow Fry Secret

  1. Check the Oil Temp: Heat deep-frying oil in a wide kadai. The oil must be low-medium hot (around 175°C / 350°F). Drop a tiny piece of dough into it—it should sink and rise slowly with small bubbles. If it pops up instantly, the oil is too hot.
  2. Gently Fry: Slide 4 to 5 shaped samosas gently into the oil. Lower the stove flame immediately to medium-low.
  3. Be Patient: Fry them slowly without turning for the first few minutes until the outer skin firms up. Flip occasionally and let them fry for 10 to 12 minutes. Frying slowly ensures the inside cooks and eliminates bubbles on the outer crust.
  4. Drain: Once the crust achieves a uniform golden-brown, crisp texture, scoop them out using a slotted spoon and drain them over paper towels.

Serve your flaky Punjabi Samosas steaming hot alongside sweet, tangy tamarind chutney and spicy mint-coriander chutney!
